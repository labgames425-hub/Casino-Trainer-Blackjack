Privacy Policy
Effective date: May 14, 2026

Blackjack Pro ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile blackjack game for entertainment and skill practice. The App does not offer real-money gambling, cash prizes, or wagering with real currency.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Blackjack Pro, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect
Gameplay and App Data
The App may store gameplay and profile information, including:

Bankroll, chip balances, loans, rewards, table unlocks, cosmetics, and card-back choices
Current round state, saved hands, bets, payouts, and game mode progress
Lesson progress, strategy drill results, win/loss records, streaks, and gameplay statistics
Local identifiers used to remember your profile and prevent duplicate purchase rewards
Most of this information is stored locally on your device. Some gameplay information may also be sent to our backend services for leaderboards, daily rewards, economy state, analytics, and app functionality.

Leaderboard and Game Center Data
If you use leaderboards or Game Center features, the App may collect and display:

Player display name
Player identifier
Avatar or cosmetic selection
Bankroll score, win streak, rank, and leaderboard category
Date or daily leaderboard period
Leaderboard information may be visible to other players in the App.

Purchases
The App offers optional in-app purchases through Apple. Apple processes payments and billing information. We do not receive or store your payment card number.

The App may store purchase-related information needed to deliver and protect purchased items, such as:

Product identifier
Transaction identifier
Purchase status
Rewards granted in the App
Analytics
The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how players use features such as gameplay, shop, stats, campaign lessons, strategy tools, and leaderboards. Analytics may include app events, device information, approximate usage information, and diagnostic data.

We use analytics to improve gameplay balance, fix issues, understand feature usage, and make the App better.

Ads and Tracking
The App uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads may allow you to receive in-game benefits, such as extra virtual chips or support rewards.

AdMob and related Google services may collect information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, ad interactions, approximate location, diagnostics, and other data used to provide, measure, and improve ads.

Where required, the App asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before allowing tracking for personalized advertising. You can allow or deny this permission. The App remains playable if you deny tracking, though ads may be less personalized.

You can change tracking permission later in iOS Settings.

Information You Provide
If you contact us for support, we may collect the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, device details, screenshots, or other information needed to respond.

How We Use Information
We use information to:

Run the App and provide gameplay features
Save progress, stats, table unlocks, cosmetics, and rewards
Process and deliver in-app purchases
Provide daily rewards, loans, and leaderboard features
Display and measure optional rewarded ads
Understand feature usage and improve the App
Detect errors, troubleshoot issues, and prevent abuse
Respond to support requests
Comply with legal, platform, and App Store requirements
Sharing of Information
We may share information with service providers that help operate the App, including:

Apple, for App Store distribution, Game Center, and in-app purchases
Google AdMob, for advertising and ad measurement
Firebase and Google Cloud, for analytics, backend services, leaderboards, remote functionality, and app operations
These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, to enforce our terms, to prevent fraud or abuse, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

Your Choices
You can control certain data uses through your device and App Store settings:

Tracking: manage tracking permission in iOS Settings.
Game Center: manage Game Center account and visibility in iOS Settings.
Ads: manage Apple and Google ad personalization choices through your device and Google account settings.
Purchases: manage subscriptions and purchase history through your Apple ID. Blackjack Pro currently uses consumable in-app purchases.
Local data: deleting the App removes local data stored on your device.
Some leaderboard, analytics, purchase-delivery, and backend records may not be removed by deleting the App from your device.

Data Retention
We keep information for as long as needed to provide the App, operate leaderboards and rewards, maintain purchase integrity, analyze performance, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is reset, overwritten, or deleted with the App. Daily leaderboard data may reset on a daily schedule, but backend logs, analytics, diagnostics, and purchase records may be retained longer by us or our service providers.

Children
Blackjack Pro is not directed to children under 13. The App is intended for entertainment and skill practice and includes simulated blackjack gameplay. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

International Transfers
Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. These locations may have data protection laws different from those in your region.

Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
